## About the course

This course rebuilds the 3D modelling workflow around Blender and AI assistance. Learners turn a design requirement into an AI-ready brief, generate geometry with AI-assisted Blender Python, then analyse, repair and refine the models before preparing them for rendering, game/visualisation export and 3D printing.

The trainer deck is concept-led and visual. Detailed click-by-click procedures live in the Learner Guide and in each activity folder.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Devise metrics for AI-assisted 3D design outputs and formulate solutions to modelling problems.
- **LO2:** Analyse Blender models for accuracy, topology quality and alignment with design requirements.
- **LO3:** Review and prepare 3D assets for organisational needs, applicable standards and production delivery.

## Topics covered

1. **Generative AI for 3D Design Concepts and Modeling** - AI-assisted 3D workflow, model briefs, prompt architecture for geometry, reference images, the Blender scene and data model, units and scale, mesh anatomy, primitive blockout, non-destructive modifiers, Blender Python (`bpy`), error-feedback iteration, design variation, provenance and responsible use.
2. **AI-Assisted 3D Model Analysis, Validation and Refinement** - validation framework, silhouette and proportion, topology quality, manifold geometry, normals, transform hygiene, cleanup and retopology, polygon budgets and LOD, UVs and seams, PBR materials, AI-generated textures, lighting for validation and change control.
3. **3D Model Optimization, Standards and Production Workflows** - target-use matrix, technical conventions, dimensions and tolerances, render engines, camera and composition, colour management, export formats, game-ready optimisation, 3D-print preparation, animation-ready organisation, documentation, automated checks, stakeholder review and final release.
