# Activity 7: Retopologize and Optimize an AI Mesh for Its Target Use

**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced
**Alignment:** K2 · K4 · A3 · A4

## Scenario

A dense generated mesh must become a responsive real-time asset without losing its recognizable silhouette.

## Objective

Select a retopology approach, meet a polygon budget and preserve critical form.

## Output

A target-use retopology, statistics comparison and feature-retention evidence.
